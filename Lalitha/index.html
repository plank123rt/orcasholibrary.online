Pre-pregnancy, active conception, and pregnancy itself aren't separate protocols — they're one continuous window. What we build in the small intestine now keeps paying out even if conception happens tomorrow.

02 — Program Philosophy

Built to work across all three states at once

This protocol doesn't assume a single outcome. It's designed so the same three months of work stay useful no matter which of the three states you're in when they end.

Even if conception happens immediately, the new supplements selected for the next three months remain fully supportive — nothing needs to be re-thought or restarted. At the same time, the program is structured to counterbalance the gastrointestinal and systemic side effects of immunosuppressant medication, so resilience is built across organ systems simultaneously, not just in one direction.

In plain terms: one plan, three states, no wasted effort — whichever door you walk through next, the groundwork already fits.
03 — Why the Small Intestine

The interface everything else runs through

The small intestine is the primary biochemical interface between nutrition, endocrine signaling, immune modulation, and nervous system regulation. It's not one input among many — it's the shared gateway all of them pass through.

Optimizing that interface has a direct line to menstruation, fertility, implantation, placental development, and fetal growth. Improve the gateway, and every downstream system gets cleaner signal.

04 — pH, Enzymes & Villi

Correct pH is where the whole chain starts

Optimized intraluminal pH creates the conditions for maximal enzyme activity, which drives efficient macronutrient hydrolysis. Each link only works if the one before it holds.

Correct pH → ↑ Enzyme Kinetics → ↑ Nutrient Liberation → ↑ Absorptive Efficiency

And healthy villi morphology is what turns that liberated nutrient pool into something the body can actually use — increased surface area means more efficient absorption of ions, straight through the Gastrotrack wall and into circulation.

05 — Menstrual Cycle

Stable absorption, stable cycle

Enhanced nutrient absorption stabilizes plasma micronutrient levels, which in turn improves synthesis of FSH, LH, estrogen, and progesterone.

plasma micronutrients ↑ → FSH · LH · estrogen · progesterone ↑

The same stability also reduces uterine smooth muscle spasm, while optimizing red blood cell turnover and reducing fatigue — the cycle runs smoother because the raw materials behind it are finally consistent.

06 — Fertility & Ovulation

Better inputs, better timing

The goal here is direct: improve oocyte maturation and ovulatory timing. And because inflammation that originates in the Gastrotrack doesn't stay local, reducing it improves uterine receptivity and implantation potential as well.

07 — Pregnancy & Fetal Development

The same absorption, now supporting two

Efficient intestinal absorption supports placental vascularization and fetal structural development, and reduces inflammatory pregnancy risks — the same interface from Section 3, now working on behalf of two physiological systems at once.

08 — Nervous System & Vagal Tone

Calmer Gastrotrack, calmer signaling

Improved digestion reduces visceral stress and supports parasympathetic dominance — the "rest and digest" state. That shift benefits hormonal rhythms and uterine perfusion, closing the loop between nervous system regulation and reproductive function.
