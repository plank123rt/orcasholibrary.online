A gentle word before you start

This guide shares general movement education, not a diagnosis or a medical treatment plan. Groin discomfort can come from many different causes, so it is worth having a doctor or physiotherapist take a look before you begin, especially to rule anything else out.

Please pause and see a doctor if the pain is sudden or severe, keeps getting worse, wakes you at night, or comes with a lump, fever, swelling or numbness. Within the exercises, always stay in a gentle, comfortable range and stop anything that causes sharp pain.
