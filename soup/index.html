Spinach, Mushroom & Broccoli Soup

A creamy, savory vegetable soup with a bowl of fresh guacamole on the side for a cool, bright contrast.

🍲 40 min 🌱 vegetarian 🔥 4 servings
4 servings

Ingredients

Tap an item to check it off as you prep.

For the soup base

  • 2 tbspbutter
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 1medium onion, chopped
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 Lvegetable broth
  • 120 mlheavy cream (optional)
  • 1 tspsalt
  • ½ tspblack pepper

The vegetables

  • 250 gmushrooms, sliced
  • 300 gbroccoli florets
  • 150 gbaby spinach

For the guacamole

  • 2ripe avocados
  • 1lime, juiced
  • 1small tomato, diced
  • ¼small red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbspcilantro, chopped
  • ¼ tspsalt

Guacamole is best made fresh, right before serving. Start it while the soup simmers so both land warm and cool together.

Make the soup

Exact steps, in order.

  1. Sauté the aromatics

    Melt the butter and olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more, until fragrant.

    ≈ 5 min

  2. Cook the mushrooms

    Add the sliced mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and start to brown at the edges.

    ≈ 6 min

  3. Add broccoli and broth

    Stir in the broccoli florets and pour in the vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the broccoli is fork-tender.

    ≈ 12 min

  4. Wilt the spinach

    Stir in the baby spinach and cook just until it wilts down into the soup.

    ≈ 2 min

  5. Blend (optional)

    Blend the soup partially or fully with an immersion blender for a creamy texture, or leave it chunky and rustic.

  6. Finish the soup

    Stir in the cream if using, then season with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust before serving.

Make the guacamole

  1. Mash the avocados in a bowl to your preferred texture, chunky or smooth.
  2. Mix in the lime juice, diced tomato, red onion, and cilantro.
  3. Season with salt, taste, and adjust the lime and salt as needed.
  4. Serve alongside the hot soup in a small bowl.

Notes

For a vegan version, skip the cream and use olive oil in place of butter. A pinch of cumin or smoked paprika in the guacamole adds extra depth, and a squeeze of lime over the finished soup brightens the whole bowl.

Serves 4 · Best eaten the day it's made
