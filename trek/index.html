General idea vs. what's real
What people think
The nervous system sends more blood to your hands to cool you down
What's actually true
There's no "send blood" nerve to the hand. Only a "hold back" nerve - heat just switches it off

Zooming out · the whole-body picture
Brain - senses core temp, not skin temp
Sympathetic nerve - dials skin vessels body-wide
Blood - redirected outward to dump heat
Same mechanism as the hand, just wider: as core temperature climbs, the hypothalamus eases the one "hold-tight" nerve signal it has for skin vessels - everywhere at once - so blood shifts from the core toward the surface to shed heat.

One thermostat, whole body
The hypothalamus tracks core temp, not hand temp. When it rises, it eases the same "hold-tight" sympathetic signal everywhere skin vessels exist - scalp, face, torso, arms, legs, hands.
